Device configuration for Meizu 21 PRO
======================================

The Meizu 21 PRO (codenamed _"meizu21Pro"_) is a high-end smartphone from Meizu.

It was announced in February 2024 and released on March 2, 2024.

## Device specifications

Basic   | Spec Sheet
-------:|:-------------------------
SoC     | Qualcomm SM8650-AB Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 (4 nm)
CPU     | Octa-core (1x3.3 GHz Cortex-X4 & 3x3.2 GHz Cortex-A720 & 2x3.0 GHz Cortex-A720 & 2x2.3 GHz Cortex-A520)
GPU     | Adreno 750
Memory  | 12/16 GB LPDDR5X RAM
Shipped Android Version | 14.0 with Flyme OS 10
Storage | 256/512/1024 GB UFS 4.0
Battery | Non-removable Li-Po 5050 mAh
Display | 6.79-inch BOE Q9 LTPO AMOLED, 1368 x 3192 pixels, 21:9 ratio, 1-120Hz, HDR10+, 600 nits (typ), 1250 nits (HBM)
Rear Camera | 50 MP (Wide) + 13 MP (Ultra-wide) + 10 MP (3x Telephoto)
Front Camera | 32 MP
Fingerprint | Under-display wide-area ultrasonic fingerprint sensor
Charging | 80W wired, 50W wireless, 10W reverse wireless
WLAN | Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac/ax, dual-band
Bluetooth | Bluetooth 5.4
NFC | Yes
IR Blaster | Yes
